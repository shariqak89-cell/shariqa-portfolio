04 / Delhi AI Grind

AI for Clean Rivers

A student innovation project for the Smart City & Civic Services challenge.

School level → District level → City level

Progressing with a real civic idea

I participated in Delhi AI Grind 2025–26 and progressed from the school level to the district level and then to the city-level final stage. The final result is awaited.

My project focused on river pollution. I explored how AI, sensors and regular monitoring could help keep rivers cleaner, reduce plastic waste and protect public health and aquatic life.

Project case study

Making river cleaning smarter and more regular

The idea combines floating cleaning machines, water-quality sensors, a mobile dashboard for live updates, AI-based pollution analysis and recycling support.
